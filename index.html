Strut injectors in a supersonic scramjet combustor

A numerical study of four strut-base injector geometries in the DLR scramjet combustor, run in ANSYS Fluent.

I compared combustion efficiency and total-pressure loss across all four designs to find which geometry suits which operating condition.
