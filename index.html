B.Tech dissertation · Jan — May 2026

Strut injectors in a supersonic scramjet combustor

A numerical study of four strut-base injector geometries in a Mach 2.5 DLR scramjet combustor, run in ANSYS Fluent.

A 45° outward-angled base gave the highest exit combustion efficiency (98.8%) but also the highest total-pressure loss coefficient (0.55). A semi-circular base gave the lowest loss (0.50).
